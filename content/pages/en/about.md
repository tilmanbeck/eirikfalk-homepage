I am a tenor based in Darmstadt. Oratorio is my home: the Evangelist in Bach's Passions, the tenor parts in Handel's Messiah and Haydn's Creation, the cantatas that move between the church year and the concert hall. On the opera stage I am drawn to Mozart's lyric roles, and I secretly dream of Wagner.

I studied voice and vocal pedagogy at the Akademie für Tonkunst in Darmstadt and at the Hochschule für Kirchenmusik in Bayreuth. Since then I have performed as a soloist and as an ensemble singer in projects large and small, in Germany and abroad, from chamber forces to full concert choir.

Alongside singing I teach in Darmstadt-Bessungen. For me the two belong together: working with voices every day changes how you listen, on stage as well.
