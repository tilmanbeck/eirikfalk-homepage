## Hosting

This website is hosted by an external provider (placeholder: Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA, or GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you access the pages, technically necessary data such as IP address, date and time of access, page requested and browser type are processed in server log files. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in providing the website securely and reliably. A data processing agreement is in place with the provider.

## Audience measurement

A privacy-friendly, cookie-free method is used to evaluate visitor numbers (placeholder: Vercel Web Analytics or a comparable service). No cookies are set and no personal profiles are created. IP addresses are not stored.

## Fonts

All fonts are served from our own server. No connections to Google Fonts or other font providers are made.

## Embedded content

Videos and audio samples from third parties (for example YouTube or SoundCloud) are only loaded when you activate them by clicking. Only then is data transmitted to the respective provider. Until then you only see a preview image.

## Calendar and news feed

When you subscribe to the concert calendar or the feed, your calendar or feed reader fetches the file from our server at regular intervals. The data mentioned under Hosting is processed in the course of this.

## Contact

If you contact us by email or phone, your details are stored to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
